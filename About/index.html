About Al Mehran Events Management

Creating memorable experiences through exceptional catering and event services for over 30 years

Our Story

A journey of passion, dedication, and culinary excellence

At Al Mehran Events Management, we believe that every moment worth celebrating deserves to be unforgettable. Since our humble beginning in 1996, what started as a small family venture has grown into a trusted name in event planning and management, known for turning visions into reality with heart, creativity, and precision.

Our journey began with a passion for creating beautiful experiences not just through food, but through the thoughtful details that make an event truly special. From weddings and birthdays to corporate galas and community gatherings, we handle everything from concept to execution, so you can focus on enjoying the moment.

Over the years, we’ve had the honor of being part of life’s most cherished milestones. And with every event, we’ve carried forward the same core values that started it all: care, commitment, and connection. Whether it's designing a dreamy décor, managing vendors, crafting custom menus, or simply being there to ensure everything runs smoothly. we do it all with a personal touch.

At Al Mehran Events Management, it’s not just about managing an event. It’s about creating a feeling, a memory, an experience that stays with you long after the last guest leaves.
